Polymath Trivia

Hard trivia across science, history, geography, politics, culture, sport, tech, anime, film, music, language and money. Works offline.

Play together

Host a live game on a TV or laptop and everyone answers on their phone: Polymath Live.

Support

Found a wrong answer, a typo or a bug? Email polymathtrivia@outlook.com with the question text and we’ll fix it in the next update.

Your data

Every quiz works offline without an account. An account is optional and only used for Friends. Read the privacy policy.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, friends and online scores. Can’t get into the app? Email polymathtrivia@outlook.com from the address you signed up with and we’ll delete it for you.
