Polymath Trivia privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Polymath Trivia is a trivia game for iPhone. It is designed to work entirely on your device, and it does not collect personal information.

What the app stores

Your display name, scores, points, game history and settings are saved only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information. You can also clear it at any time from Profile, then Reset profile.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Internet use

Every quiz is built into the app and works offline. When your phone is online, the app downloads the latest weekly “Friday 50” quiz file from GitHub Pages. That request contains nothing about you beyond what any internet request includes, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or use it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
