Polymath Trivia privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Polymath Trivia is a trivia game for iPhone. Every quiz works on your device without an account. If you choose to create an account to play with friends, the app stores a small amount of information online, described below. We don’t sell it, share it for advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

Without an account

Your display name, scores, points, game history and settings are saved only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them at any time from Profile, then Reset profile.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional and are only used for the Friends features: adding friends, sending challenges and comparing scores. When you create one, we store:

  • Your email address and password, used only so you can sign in. Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form. We don’t send marketing email.
  • Your username, which other players can search for to send you a friend request, and which your friends see.
  • Your friends list and pending friend requests.
  • Game results you share with friends: your total points, your Daily ten and Friday 50 scores, and the scores and question sets of challenges you send or receive.

Only you and your accepted friends can see your scores. Other players can only find you by typing your exact username. Your email address is never shown to other players.

This information is stored by our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes it on our behalf and only to run the service.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your email address, password, username, friends, challenges and online scores straight away. Your stats saved on the phone stay until you delete the app or reset your profile. If you can’t access the app, email us and we’ll delete your account for you.

What the app does not collect

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Internet use

When your phone is online, the app downloads the latest weekly “Friday 50” quiz file from GitHub Pages. That request contains nothing about you beyond what any internet request includes, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy statement. If you’re signed in, the app also connects to Supabase to sync your friends and scores.

Polymath Live (the multiplayer web game)

When you join a live game, the nickname you type and your answers are sent through Supabase's real-time messaging service to the other screens in that game. They are not saved to any database and disappear when the game ends. The host's screen keeps the game in the browser only until the tab is closed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
